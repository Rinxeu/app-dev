My Favorite Movie: The Maze Runner - The Death Cure
Introduction

My favorite movie is The Maze Runner: The Death Cure. It is an exciting science fiction, action, and adventure movie.

About the Movie

The story follows Thomas and his friends as they try to find a cure for a deadly disease called the Flare. They face dangerous challenges while fighting against WCKD to save their friends and humanity.

My Favorite Characters
Thomas – A brave leader who wants to save his friends.
Newt – Thomas's loyal friend who shows courage and kindness.
Minho – A strong and skilled runner who never gives up.
Brenda – A brave girl who helps Thomas and his friends.
Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because:

It has exciting action scenes.
The story is interesting and unpredictable.
It shows the importance of friendship and loyalty.
It teaches us to never give up despite challenges.
